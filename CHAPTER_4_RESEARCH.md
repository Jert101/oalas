## System Used in Christ the King College de Maranding, Inc.

The analysis of the leave of absence application process at Christ the King College de Maranding, Inc. shows that the institution previously used a traditional, manual system to manage faculty and staff leave of absence applications. This approach relied on physical record-keeping and direct interaction with administrative personnel, requiring employees to visit the HR office or submit paper forms to request leave of absences. Staff manually documented application details in logbooks or on paper forms, checked leave balances by reviewing physical records, and issued handwritten approval forms or rejection notices for leave of absence requests. Although this process appeared straightforward, it faced several challenges.

Maintaining records manually was time-consuming and susceptible to errors like misfiling, duplication, or data loss. Delays in communication, particularly during busy periods, hindered the ability to provide timely updates or address inquiries efficiently. Furthermore, manual data entry increased the chances of scheduling conflicts and inaccuracies, which harmed employee satisfaction and disrupted academic operations. The system also lacked scalability, making it difficult to cope with increasing employee demand and complex approval workflows for leave of absence applications. These limitations reduced operational efficiency and failed to meet the expectations of modern employees who preferred faster and more convenient online leave of absence application processes. While the manual system may have worked for smaller operations, adopting an online application system became essential to improve efficiency, minimize errors, and enhance employee satisfaction. This transition would also enable the institution to expand its operations effectively and stay competitive in the dynamic educational industry.

The online leave of absence application process plays a vital role in academic institutions as it significantly influences efficiency, employee satisfaction, and institutional reputation. A well-organized system ensures that faculty members, staff, and administrative personnel can easily apply for and manage their leave of absences. This helps avoid issues like scheduling conflicts and understaffing, which could result in unhappy employees and disrupted educational services.

To overcome the limitations of its manual leave of absence application system, Christ the King College de Maranding, Inc. has transitioned to a digital platform called the Online Academic Leave Application System (OALAS). This system implements user-friendly online leave of absence application software that streamlines processes, centralizes applications, and enables availability checks and confirmations. The online leave of absence application portal allows employees to request leave of absences conveniently, reducing reliance on phone or in-person interactions. The cloud-based system replaces paper records, improving data security and transparency while minimizing errors in leave of absence application processing.

As mentioned by Smith and Johnson (2019), in their study entitled "Digital Transformation in Human Resource Management: Evidence from Educational Institutions," digital leave management systems lead to significant improvements in operational efficiency, reduced administrative overhead, and enhanced employee satisfaction. Their research of 150 educational institutions found that organizations implementing automated leave management systems experienced an average 60% reduction in processing time and a 40% decrease in administrative errors.

## Algorithm Used in Validation Strategies

Leave of absence application validation strategies rely on algorithms to process applications based on factors like leave balances, scheduling conflicts, and institutional policies. These include real-time validation for immediate feedback, conflict detection algorithms to identify overlapping leave periods, leave balance verification to ensure sufficient entitlements, and policy compliance checking to enforce institutional rules. Additional strategies such as exemption logic for special leave types, multi-stage approval routing, automated notifications, and audit trail generation help institutions customize validation rules, optimize processing efficiency, and satisfy employee expectations. These data-driven techniques improve validation accuracy and foster institutional compliance.

Validation strategies play a vital role in online leave of absence application systems by ensuring data integrity, preventing conflicts, and maintaining institutional standards. These strategies validate applications according to leave policies, availability, and employee entitlements, allowing institutions to optimize operations during high-demand periods while offering fair and consistent processing. Approaches like real-time, conflict-based, and policy-based validation provide flexibility, while tiered and role-based validation caters to different organizational needs.

The study conducted by Shruti Sadashiv Dhulugade et al. (2024) focused on the development and validation algorithms for leave management systems using modern web technologies. The research highlighted the potential of automated leave management systems to promote efficient processing and contribute to institutional productivity. The study developed a comprehensive validation model that considered factors such as leave type compatibility, scheduling conflicts, employee entitlements, and approval workflows. Through a sensitivity analysis, the researchers identified the factors that had the most significant impact on validation accuracy. Based on the findings, a multi-layered validation strategy was proposed to increase processing efficiency and enhance employee satisfaction. The study provided valuable insights into validation algorithms and conflict detection strategies in the leave management service industry, offering guidance for optimizing processing efficiency and employee experience in educational institutions.
