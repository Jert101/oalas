3. **Offline Functionality**: Future researchers may also develop offline functionality for the system, enabling employees to submit leave applications and administrators to manage approvals even in areas with poor internet connectivity, ensuring continuous operation during network disruptions.

4. **System Enhancements**: Future improvements may also include advanced notification systems, adding mobile application support to provide users with convenient access from smartphones and tablets, and implementing automation for recurring tasks such as sending reminders for pending applications, generating leave balance reports, and updating institutional policies automatically.
