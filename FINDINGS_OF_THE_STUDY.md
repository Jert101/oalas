# FINDINGS OF THE STUDY

The following findings were revealed based on the research questions addressed in this study:

## 1. Current Challenges in Manual Leave Management at Christ the King College de Maranding, Inc.

The research found that Christ the King College de Maranding, Inc. was relying on a manual system for managing leave of absence applications. The manual system, which relied on physical record-keeping, paper-based forms, and direct interaction with administrative personnel, caused significant inefficiencies and frequent errors. Employees had to manually manage leave applications and track leave balances, leading to issues like scheduling conflicts, double bookings, and missed applications. Mistakes during data entry, such as incorrect employee information and balance calculation errors, further complicated the process. The system was also slow, causing delays in confirming applications and updating leave balances, which negatively impacted the employee experience and institutional operations. These challenges hindered the institution's ability to grow and effectively manage faculty and staff leave requests, highlighting the need for a more efficient, automated solution.

## 2. Efficiency Improvements Through the Online Application of Leave of Absence System

The automated system was developed to address these gaps, creating user-friendly features for both employees and administrators. This enabled online leave applications, real-time tracking of leave balances, and automated approval confirmations. Automation reduced errors, expedited application processing, and streamlined administrative workflows, leading to better user experiences and more efficient institutional operations. The system achieved a 60% reduction in processing time compared to manual methods, with a mean efficiency score of 4.54 (Very Efficient) across all operational dimensions. The system provided real-time capabilities with instant access to application status and leave balances, automated approval routing and notification systems that significantly reduced administrative overhead, and demonstrated exceptional capability in managing peak application periods with a mean score of 4.55. Seamless integration with existing academic management systems achieved a mean score of 4.70, ensuring efficient data exchange and operational continuity.

## 3. Key Features and Strategies for Enhanced Functionality and Usability

The study proposed using advanced validation algorithms and automated approval workflows to enhance leave management strategies. The functionality enhancements included user-friendly application forms that achieved a mean score of 4.75, real-time leave balance management that scored 4.75, automated approval workflows with multi-stage processes and role-based access control, comprehensive reporting that achieved 4.65, and secure authentication for different user types including faculty, staff, administrators, and department heads. The usability improvements featured intuitive navigation that scored 4.25, clean interface design that achieved 4.65, multi-level user support that scored 4.55, real-time responsiveness that achieved 4.65, and visual appeal that scored 4.40. The technical strategies implemented included validation algorithms for real-time conflict detection, exemption logic for special leave types, automated notifications for status updates, audit trail generation for comprehensive tracking, and cross-platform compatibility for multiple devices.

## 4. ISO 9126-1 Standard Evaluation Results

The study used the ISO 9126-1 standard evaluation tool to assess the system's performance in several key areas. It was found to be reliable, consistently performing well for both employees and administrators, efficient in managing online leave applications with real-time leave balance information and transparent approval processes, and user-friendly with easy navigation for employees, faculty, staff, and administrators. The quality assessment results revealed that the system achieved a reliability mean score of 4.44 (Very Reliable), efficiency mean score of 4.54 (Very Efficient), functionality mean score of 4.63 (Very Functional), usability mean score of 4.50 (Very Usable), maintainability mean score of 4.55 (Very Maintainable), and portability mean score of 4.68 (Very Portable). The system achieved an overall mean score of 4.56, indicating Very High Quality across all ISO 9126-1 characteristics, significantly exceeding industry standards for educational software systems.

## Summary of Key Findings

The comprehensive evaluation of the Online Application for Leave of Absence System (OALAS) revealed that the system successfully addresses all identified challenges in the manual leave management process at Christ the King College de Maranding, Inc. The system demonstrates exceptional performance across all quality metrics, with Very High Quality ratings in reliability, efficiency, functionality, usability, maintainability, and portability. The implementation of automated workflows, real-time processing, and user-friendly interfaces has resulted in significant improvements in operational efficiency, employee satisfaction, and administrative effectiveness. The system's proven capabilities position it as an ideal solution for modernizing leave management operations in educational institutions.
